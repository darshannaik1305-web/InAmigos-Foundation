Who We Are

Empowering Communities, Inspiring Change

Founded on September 23, 2020, by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a Central Government licensed non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh. The foundation is 80G & 12A certified, CSR-1 registered, NITI Aayog registered, and IAF ISO 9001:2015 certified. Through volunteer-driven initiatives, the organization focuses on education support, food drives, environmental conservation, women empowerment, and animal welfare.

Our Mission

To create meaningful social impact by supporting underprivileged communities through education, sustainability, food support, women empowerment, and volunteer-driven initiatives.

Our Vision

To build a compassionate, inclusive, and empowered society where every individual has access to opportunities, dignity, and support.
